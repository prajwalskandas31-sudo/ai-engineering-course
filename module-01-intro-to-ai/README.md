## What you'll learn

- What AI is, and how it differs from human intelligence
- History and evolution of AI, from the 1943 neuron model to Generative AI
- Types of AI: Narrow, General and Super AI, plus the functional types
- AI vs Machine Learning vs Deep Learning
- The AI life cycle
- Real-world applications and the future of AI
